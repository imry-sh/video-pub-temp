# Privacy Policy – "video pub" (Gal Einai)

Last updated: November 23, 2026

"video pub" is an internal tool used only by the Gal Einai team to publish our own videos to Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts, YouTube channels and TikTok accounts that we own and manage.

**What data the app accesses.** The app uses access tokens granted by our own administrators to upload videos, captions and cover images to our own pages and accounts, and to read basic information about those pages (name, ID, posts and their engagement counts). It does not collect data about other users, visitors or followers.

**How data is used and stored.** Tokens are stored privately and used only to publish and manage our own content. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

**Data deletion.** Because the app is used only by our own administrators, no third-party user data is stored. To request deletion of any data, or to revoke access, email us at the address listed on our website, or remove the app in Facebook Settings > Apps and Websites. We will delete any related data within 30 days.

**Contact.** Gal Einai – https://www.inner.org
