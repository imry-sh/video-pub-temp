# Data Deletion Instructions – "video pub" (Gal Einai)

"video pub" is an internal publishing tool used only by the Gal Einai team on its own pages and accounts. It does not store personal data of other Facebook users.

To remove the app and any data related to you:
1. Go to Facebook Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites.
2. Find "video pub" and click Remove.

You can also request deletion through the contact details at https://www.inner.org. We will delete any related data within 30 days.
